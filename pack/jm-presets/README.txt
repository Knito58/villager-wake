JourneyMap group presets and keybinds (Villager Wake)

groups.preset.dat   Waypoint group definitions (Lava Lakes, Base, Temp = white cross, ...).
jm-copy-groups.py   Copies them into a world. Needs Python 3. Close Minecraft first.
                    python3 -I jm-copy-groups.py        (lists worlds in ../saves, you pick one)
keybinds.options.txt  The key_ lines of the author's options.txt. To use them, close the game and
                    copy these lines into your own options.txt (replace the matching key_ lines).
                    It is not applied automatically, so your other settings are never overwritten.
